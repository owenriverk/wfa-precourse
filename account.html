Sync your progress

The course never requires an account. Your progress lives in this browser. If you want it to follow you — phone on the trail, laptop at home — turn on sync: we email you a sign-in link, then keep a backup of your lesson progress, quiz scores, kit checklist, and exam record.

Turn on sync

Enter your email and we'll send a one-time sign-in link. No password. We store your email address and your progress — nothing else — and you can delete both with one click.

Sync is on

Signed in as . This device syncs automatically as you study. Sign in with the same email on another device and your progress merges — the best score from each device wins.

Delete synced data

Removes your email and synced progress from our server everywhere, immediately. Progress already stored in this browser stays here.

Sync isn't available on this mirror

You're viewing a copy of the site that serves files only. Use openwfa.com for progress sync — the course itself works the same everywhere.
